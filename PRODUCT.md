# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Citizens of Thimphu use CivicSense to report municipal issues, attach supporting photos, track complaint status, and
maintain their account details. Municipal administrators review and triage complaints, manage their administrator
profile, and invite additional administrators to the service.

## Product Purpose

CivicSense Bhutan is a Streamlit and SQLite prototype for citizen complaint reporting and municipal triage. Success
means citizens can submit and follow up on reports, while authorized municipal staff can review complaints and
coordinate responses with clear role-based access.

## Brand Personality

Practical, trustworthy, and accessible. The interface should support residents and municipal staff completing
important service tasks with clear language and predictable controls.

## Anti-references

Avoid horizontal sidebar navigation, oversized sidebars, modal dialogs for routine tasks, confusing role changes,
and public administrator registration.

## Design Principles

- Keep citizen reporting and municipal administration workflows distinct and easy to find.
- Make security-sensitive actions explicit, authenticated, and understandable.
- Prefer compact, predictable navigation and direct feedback after actions.
- Preserve accessible keyboard operation, visible focus, and readable contrast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus indicators, readable contrast, and clear labels. Keep navigation
usable at narrow viewport widths and do not rely on color alone to communicate status.
